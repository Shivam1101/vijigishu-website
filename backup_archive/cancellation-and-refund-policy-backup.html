1. General Terms & Conditions

Fundamental guidelines governing immersion operations and passenger responsibilities

  • Force Majeure & Itinerary Modifications: The company holds the right to make changes in the program itinerary due to force majeure, unforeseen circumstances, natural calamities, civil unrest, or situations beyond human control. The company will not be liable for losses incurred due to situations leading to the termination or postponement of the immersion program.
  • MOU & Mutual Consent: Any change or waiver to the terms of the institutional MOU or its conditions shall be made only with the mutual written consent of both partnering institutions and VEPL. Neither party may amend terms unilaterally.
  • Amendments in Terms: Any change in VEPL Terms & Conditions will be informed beforehand to the other party in writing. However, VEPL reserves the sole right to amend its terms & conditions. In case the amended version overrides previously agreed terms, the same shall be discussed and a mutually agreeable solution will take effect.
  • 100% Pre-Payment in India: All bookings must be made on a pre-payment basis (i.e. must be paid 100% in India before the passenger departs).
  • Price & Rate Revisions: All rates are subject to revision in case of sudden changes in foreign exchange rates, changes in airfare / train / bus tickets, fuel surcharge hikes, revisions in existing statutory tax structures, or sudden changes in hotel tariffs.
  • No Mid-Tour Amendments: No amendments or modifications are allowed after the commencement of the travel program or during the tour under any circumstances.
  • Airline Group Booking Non-Deviation: In case of group booking with an airline, deviations are not permitted (i.e. travelers cannot change travel dates or destinations individually). Any deviation from the customer's end will lead to cost revisions, and additional costs based on fare differences or applicable airline fares shall be paid over and above the tour cost.
  • Loss of Passport or Travel Documents: In case of loss of passport during the tour leading to changes in travel plans, extended stay, charges for re-issuing emergency certificates/landing cards, or additional logistical expenses, all costs must be borne entirely by the client/passenger.
  • Pre-Existing Medical Conditions: Travelers must inform the company in writing about any pre-existing disease or illness prior to departure. The insurance company and VEPL are not liable to pay for any medical expenses accruing from pre-existing illnesses.
  • Adherence to Schedule: All travelers must strictly adhere to the timings given by the program coordinator or mentioned in the itinerary. VEPL is not responsible for any refund or reimbursement for unutilized sightseeing activities, transfers, or tickets due to delays from any traveler.
  • Missed Connections: VEPL will not be responsible for missed connections of international or domestic flights, trains, or buses due to whatsoever reason. In such cases, travelers will be responsible for bearing the cost of new tickets or rescheduling penalties.
  • Tour Decorum & Legal Compliance: Individual travelers are strictly responsible for maintaining the decorum of the tour. Travelers must refrain from indulging in illegal activities or violating local laws of the host nation. VEPL will not be responsible for any legal action taken against any traveler for personal acts.
  • Property & Coach Damage: VEPL will not be responsible for damages caused to any property, hotel, coach, or vehicle during travel. Losses will be borne by the responsible individual traveler or collectively by the group.
  • Voluntary Tour Discontinuation: In case of voluntarily canceling or discontinuing from an ongoing tour due to any reason, VEPL will not be liable for any refunds or flight/train rescheduling charges.
  • Quarantine Costs: In case of quarantine requirements, travelers bear all accommodation and meal costs and must adhere to host country health rules.
  • Group Coordinator Nomination: For group travel, a faculty or administrative coordinator shall be nominated by the respective institute/university, and coordinator contact details must be shared with VEPL.

2. Itinerary & Booking Confirmation

Milestones for itinerary sign-off and seat confirmation

Itinerary Governance

  • VEPL shall provide a draft itinerary 90 to 120 days before travel and submit the final itinerary for institutional approval at least 15 days prior to departure.
  • No changes may be made after approval without written consent. If operational changes become unavoidable, VEPL shall offer equal or higher-value alternatives at no extra cost.
  • Customers cannot request last-minute or ad-hoc changes to the established tour itinerary.

Booking Confirmation

  • A booking is confirmed only after the initial booking deposit is credited to the company's official bank account.
  • In case of delays in booking or registering beyond the stipulated deadline, VEPL cannot guarantee seat availability at the previously quoted cost. In case seats are available, cost revisions will apply.

3. Payment Terms, TCS & Bank Account

Deposit timelines, tax regulations, and official corporate payment coordinates

  • Deposit Requirement: A minimum deposit of 50% (or as quoted on the program proposal) is required to hold bookings on a confirmed basis.
  • Peak Season Full Payment: During high peak seasons (Christmas, New Year, Diwali, etc.) and last-minute bookings, 100% full payment is required at the time of booking.
  • Supplier Advance Requirements: Depending on airline and overseas contractor payment deadlines, additional advance tranches may be requested prior to the final balance date.
  • Tax Collected at Source (TCS): On international tours, 5% TCS on total cost is charged extra. TCS collected reflects in Form 26AS under the provided PAN card, claimable during Income Tax Return filing. Without PAN card submission, 10% TCS is applicable. TCS does not apply if the institutional buyer deducts TDS under applicable provisions.
  • Balance Payment: Full balance payment must be credited 15 days prior to departure. Payment delays may lead to automated booking cancellation or airline fare escalation.

6. Circumstantial Interruptions & Force Majeure

Guidelines for lockdowns, travel restrictions, and uncontrollable global events

  • Government Restrictions & Lockdowns: In the event of a lockdown or government-mandated travel restrictions, the tour may be postponed to a mutually convenient date within twelve (12) months or converted into a credit note. Variations arising from third-party price changes (airfare differences, hotel seasonal tariffs) shall be transparently communicated and agreed upon before confirmation.
  • Voluntary Tour Termination Service Charge: In case the client chooses to terminate the tour and seek a cash refund where allowable, a minimum 15% service charge applies on the total cost of the tour package.
  • Non-Refundable Elements: Industrial visit fees, academic certifications, pre-booked guided sightseeing, and non-recoverable hotel/coach deposits are strictly non-refundable once committed.
  • Airlines Credit Policy: Airlines issue refunds or credit notes only if the flight is canceled by the airline. Client-initiated cancellations are governed by standard airline cancellation penalties.
  • Force Majeure Definition: Force Majeure includes Acts of God, severe natural weather events, epidemics/pandemics (including COVID-19 variations), government lockdowns, strikes, civil unrest, war, border closures, or withdrawal of travel licenses.
  • Limitation of Liability: Under Force Majeure, VEPL's obligations are suspended without breach. Any refund is strictly limited to amounts recovered from suppliers after deducting non-recoverable costs and administrative charges. VEPL may issue future travel credits valid for 12 months in lieu of cash refunds.

7. Visa Guidelines & Regulations

Consular authority, document compliance, and traveler responsibilities

  • Consular Authority: Granting or rejecting visas is at the sole discretion of the respective Embassy or Consulate. VEPL is not responsible for losses if travel plans are hampered due to visa delays or rejections.
  • Non-Refundable Visa Fees: Visa consular fees and service charges paid to authorized visa agents are 100% non-refundable under all circumstances.
  • Flight Rescheduling Due to Visa Delays: If a visa delay necessitates flight rescheduling, all fare differences and penalties are borne by the affected traveler.
  • Passport Validity: All travelers must hold a machine-readable passport with a minimum validity of six (6) months beyond the scheduled date of return to India.
  • Verification of Visa Details: Travelers must verify all details (Name, Passport Number, Date of Birth, Visa Dates) immediately upon receiving the visa and notify VEPL on the same day for rectification if necessary.
  • Application Timeline: Visas must be applied for at least one month before departure. For destinations where appointments are centrally allocated (UK/Europe), VEPL holds no responsibility for consular appointment unavailability.
  • Personal Appearance: Travelers are required to appear in person for biometric appointments or interviews at the designated Visa Application Centre / Consulate, regardless of their city of residence.
  • Premium Visa Services: Special services such as doorstep biometric pickup or premium lounge access carry extra charges to be paid directly by the traveler.

8. Refunds & Legal Jurisdiction

Disbursement protocol, RBI compliance, and governing dispute resolution

  • Refund Processing Timeline: Approved refunds for cancellations are disbursed directly to the client within a minimum of 30 working days.
  • Third-Party Supplier Timelines: Refunds from third-party partners (airlines, overseas destination management companies, cruise operators) take between 30 to 90 working days.
  • No Refund for Unutilized Services: No refunds are payable for any partially utilized or unutilized services during travel (including missed meals, unutilized hotel beds in twin/triple sharing, coach seats, or unused entrance tickets).
  • INR Disbursement & RBI Compliance: Refunds for foreign exchange components are made strictly in Indian Rupees (INR) at the exchange rate prevailing on the date of transaction, in full compliance with Reserve Bank of India (RBI) regulations.
  • No Post-Commencement Refunds: Absolutely no refunds are granted for cancellations made after the official commencement of travel.
  • Legal Jurisdiction: All contracts, agreements, and booking terms are subject exclusively to the legal jurisdiction of the courts of Pune and Mumbai, Maharashtra, India only.
