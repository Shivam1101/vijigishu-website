Who We Are

Conquer the World

Vijigishu (विजिगीषु) is a Sanskrit term meaning "World conqueror forever," perfectly capturing our visionary ethos.

We are committed to transforming lives and making a profound impact by crafting unique learning experiences through our diverse travel programs. Our expertise lies in curating International and Domestic Academic Immersion Programs tailored for school, undergraduate, and postgraduate students.

We also offer Transformational Learning Programs, Leadership Walks, Spiritual Retreats, International Internship Programs, Study Abroad initiatives, Faculty Development Programs, Summer and Winter Schools, and Seminars & Conferences — shaping the leaders of tomorrow through experiences that transcend the classroom.

Our I-RACE Philosophy

Core Values

At Vijigishu, our values define who we are, how we work, and the way we build lasting relationships with our clients, partners, and team members. Guided by our I-RACE philosophy, we are committed to maintaining the highest standards in everything we do.

I
Integrity

We believe in being ethical, sincere, and transparent in every interaction. Integrity is the foundation of our business, helping us build trust and long-term relationships.

R
Respect

We treat every individual with dignity, fairness, and respect. We value diverse perspectives and foster an environment where everyone feels heard and empowered.

A
Accountability

We take ownership of our actions and commitments. By being accountable, we ensure reliability, responsibility, and continuous improvement in all our efforts.

C
Collaboration

Success comes from working together. We encourage teamwork, open communication, and shared goals to create innovative solutions and meaningful results.

E
Excellence

We strive for excellence in every aspect of our work. Through dedication and continuous learning, we aim to exceed expectations and deliver the highest quality outcomes.

Co-Founder & Director

Mr. Pranav Chobhe
