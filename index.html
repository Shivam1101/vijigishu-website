Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of food is provided during the trip?
Indian meals are provided, with options for vegetarian, non-vegetarian, and Jain food.
Will Vijigishu assist with the visa process?
Yes, Vijigishu provides complete visa assistance for the traveling group, including documentation guidance and process support.
Can the trip be customised for our college?
Yes, all our programs are fully customizable based on your academic requirements, preferred destinations, duration, and budget.
Who will be our point of contact during the trip?
An experienced Vijigishu tour leader will travel with the group and act as the primary point of contact, ensuring smooth coordination throughout the journey.
Is travel insurance included in the program?
Yes, travel insurance is included in the program for all participants.
What kind of accommodation is provided?
We provide comfortable 3-star or 4-star category hotels, ensuring safety, convenience, and a student-friendly environment.
How do you balance learning and leisure?
Our programs combine academic sessions, industry visits, and cultural exploration with visits to iconic landmarks — ensuring students learn while enjoying the destination.
