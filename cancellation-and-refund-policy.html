  • VEPL is not responsible for any losses occurring due to delays or rejection in visa. There is no refund for Visa charges paid to the consulate/Embassy & the service charges applied by the respective authorised visa agent of the Consulate / Embassy.
  • Visa processing timelines are determined solely by the respective consulates or embassies. VEPL shall not be held liable for delays or decisions made by immigration authorities. In the event that a visa delay necessitates flight rescheduling or results in penalties, such costs shall be borne by the affected traveller. Both parties acknowledge the importance of applying for visas within the stipulated timelines prescribed by the destination country’s immigration authorities to minimize such risks.
  • All travellers must hold a machine-readable passport with a minimum validity of six (6) months beyond the date of return to India. Travelers are responsible for verifying the accuracy of their personal details on the visa, including name, passport number, and date of birth, prior to travel. Visa approval, delays, or refusals related to security checks, background issues, or immigration decisions remain solely at the discretion of the respective consulate or embassy, and VEPL shall not be held liable for such outcomes.
  • Please check your details (Name, Passport number, DOB, etc) on the visa before you travel.
  • Visa may get stuck due to security reasons or blacklisting; hence we are not responsible for any such type of cases as it is purely subject to immigration approval.
  • All personal details on the Visa should be checked by the traveler and ensure that
    • The details are correct as per the passport and they shall share it with the VEPL point of contact on the same day as received by the traveler for confirmation and rectification if required.
  • In case of special services of Visa like doorstep, premium lounge etc, extra charges will be borne by the traveler/s
  • It is pertinent to note that the finalization of travel dates as per the schedule given above is paramount for the timely processing of Visas which is linked to flight bookings as well.
  • Delay in the finalization of travel dates has a cascading effect on bookings and visa processing. Visas shall be applied at least minimum one month before the travel date or strictly as per the stipulated time frame advised by the Consulate/ Embassy of a particular country. For some countries (Especially for European countries/ UK) sometimes visa appointment dates for groups comes from Consulate/ Embassy, Vijigishu cannot guarantee or hold any responsibility for visa appointment dates/ visa processing as it is centrally controlled by respective Consulates/ Embassy.

Vijigishu will not be responsible for any changes in appointment dates or interview dates allotted by the respective Consulate/ Embassy, nor Vijigishu will bear any costs required to report / to travel to the visa application center / Consulate/ Embassy.
