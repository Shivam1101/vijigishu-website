Schedule

Indicative Itinerary

West Europe (8 Days | 7 Nights)

Day 1 – Arrival in Paris
  • Arrival in Paris and transfer to the hotel.
  • Paris city orientation covering the key landmarks and surroundings.
  • Enjoy a Seine River Cruise and experience the city from the waterfront.
  • Hotel check-in and overnight stay in Paris.
Day 2 – Industry & City Exposure
  • Breakfast followed by an industry visit / academic session.
  • Interaction with professionals / academic experts and practical learning exposure.
  • Visit the iconic Eiffel Tower.
  • Explore central Paris and its major city landmarks.
  • Overnight stay in Paris.
Day 3 – Exploration Day
  • Breakfast and a full day for independent learning and exploration.
  • Option to visit Disneyland Paris, cultural sites or local markets.
  • Experience Parisian culture, lifestyle and local surroundings.
  • Overnight stay in Paris.
Day 4 – Paris Brussels
  • Breakfast and check-out from the hotel.
  • Travel from Paris to Brussels.
  • En-route industry visit for practical exposure and interaction.
  • Arrival in Brussels and city tour covering the key landmarks.
  • Hotel check-in and overnight stay in Brussels.
Day 5 – Brussels Essen
  • Breakfast and check-out.
  • Transfer from Brussels to Essen, Germany.
  • Attend an interactive university session with academic experts.
  • Receive participation / academic certification.
  • Networking and interaction with university representatives and students.
  • Overnight stay in Essen.
Day 6 – Essen Amsterdam
  • Breakfast and check-out from the hotel.
  • Transfer from Essen to Amsterdam, Netherlands.
  • Visit a sustainability-focused project to understand practical approaches to sustainable development.
  • Hotel check-in and overnight stay in Amsterdam.
Day 7 – Amsterdam Exploration
  • Breakfast followed by an academic / industry visit.
  • Interaction with professionals and practical learning exposure.
  • Visit Zaanse Schans, a traditional Dutch village experience.
  • Explore traditional Dutch architecture, windmills and local surroundings.
  • Return to Amsterdam and overnight stay.
Day 8 – Departure
  • Breakfast and hotel check-out.
  • Free time for local exploration, subject to departure schedule.
  • Transfer to the airport.
  • Departure with valuable academic, industry and international cultural exposure.

East Europe (10–11 Days)

Day 1 – Arrival in Frankfurt
  • Arrival in Frankfurt and transfer to the hotel.
  • Cultural orientation and introduction to the city.
  • Explore the surrounding areas and key landmarks.
  • Hotel check-in and overnight stay in Frankfurt.
Day 2 – Engineering & Industry Exposure
  • Breakfast followed by a visit to an automobile / engineering hub.
  • Gain practical exposure to engineering, manufacturing and industrial processes.
  • Academic interaction with industry / academic professionals.
  • Return to the hotel and overnight stay in Frankfurt.
Day 3 – Heritage & Transition to Stuttgart
  • Breakfast and visit to historic sites in Frankfurt.
  • Check-out and transfer to Stuttgart.
  • Explore the city's heritage and cultural surroundings.
  • Visit a museum for industry / technology-oriented learning.
  • Hotel check-in and overnight stay in Stuttgart.
Day 4 – Infrastructure Learning
  • Breakfast followed by airport operations / infrastructure exposure.
  • Understand airport systems, operations and infrastructure management.
  • Explore Stuttgart city and its key landmarks.
  • Return to the hotel and overnight stay in Stuttgart.
Day 5 – Stuttgart Munich
  • Breakfast and check-out from the hotel.
  • Transfer from Stuttgart to Munich.
  • Cultural and architectural exploration of Munich.
  • Explore the city's historic landmarks and urban surroundings.
  • Hotel check-in and overnight stay in Munich.
Day 6 – Industry & Systems Exposure
  • Breakfast followed by a visit to a manufacturing / industry unit.
  • Gain insights into industrial systems, operations and management practices.
  • Interaction with industry professionals.
  • Visit Allianz Arena and explore its architecture and operations.
  • Overnight stay in Munich.
Day 7 – Travel to Prague
  • Breakfast and check-out from the hotel.
  • Travel from Munich to Prague, Czech Republic.
  • Arrival in Prague and hotel check-in.
  • Prague city orientation and cultural exploration.
  • Overnight stay in Prague.
Day 8 – Prague: Architecture & Urban Learning
  • Breakfast followed by an exploration of Prague's architecture and urban design.
  • Visit key architectural and historic sites.
  • Academic learning through observation of the city's urban planning and heritage.
  • Cultural exploration of Prague.
  • Overnight stay in Prague.
Day 9 – Prague Vienna
  • Breakfast and check-out from the hotel.
  • Transfer from Prague to Vienna, Austria.
  • Vienna city tour covering major landmarks and cultural attractions.
  • Explore the city's architecture, heritage and urban environment.
  • Hotel check-in and overnight stay in Vienna.
Day 10 – Vienna Budapest
  • Breakfast and check-out from the hotel.
  • Transfer from Vienna to Budapest, Hungary.
  • Arrival and city exploration covering key landmarks.
  • Experience Budapest's architecture, culture and cityscape.
  • Overnight stay in Budapest.
Day 11 – Departure
  • Breakfast and hotel check-out.
  • Free time for local exploration, subject to departure schedule.
  • Transfer to the airport.
  • Departure with valuable international academic, industry, engineering and cultural exposure.
