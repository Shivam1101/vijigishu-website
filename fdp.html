Special Learning Tours - Faculty Development Program

The Faculty Development Program (FDP) provides a unique opportunity for faculty members to collaborate with top-tier international institutions and immerse themselves in the latest education, management, and architecture trends.

What We Offer

  • Networking opportunities with entrepreneurs, and top level management executives of leading global companies.
  • Interactive workshops in top QS-ranked universities and leading global institutions across Europe and Asia.
  • Participate in workshops, seminars, and hands-on learning experiences
  • Engage in peer-to-peer learning and a cultural exchange

Program Highlights

  • Explore emerging technologies such as Drone Technology and Artificial Intelligence, along with hands-on exposure to modern AI tools and industry applications.
  • Engage in discussions around Management & Entrepreneurship, Strategic Management, Innovation, Growth Mindset, and Design Thinking to address modern-day challenges and opportunities.
  • Interact with academicians from international universities, gain valuable industry insights, and experience meaningful cultural and local engagement.
  • Participate in mindfulness and stress management sessions designed to promote personal well-being, productivity, and work-life balance.

Connecting Indian classrooms to global boardrooms. Experiential learning journeys crafted for the next generation of leaders.
