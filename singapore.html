Discover the Future of

Recognized as one of the world's most innovative and future-ready nations, Singapore offers students a unique blend of academic excellence, industry exposure, and cultural diversity.

Through immersive learning experiences, students gain insights into global business ecosystems, smart city development, sustainability practices, and innovation-driven industries while exploring one of Asia's most dynamic destinations.

Schedule

Indicative Itinerary

Day 1 – Mumbai → Singapore | Arrival & City Exploration
  • Depart from Mumbai International Airport for Singapore
  • Arrive at Singapore Changi Airport
  • Meet & greet by local representative
  • Transfer to hotel and check-in
  • Guided city tour covering: Merlion Park, Singapore Flyer (Photo Stop), Orchard Road, Chinatown, Suntec City
  • Overnight stay at the hotel
Day 2 – Academic Engagement & Industry Immersion
  • Breakfast at the hotel
  • Academic session at a leading international institution
  • Interactive learning and global knowledge exchange
  • Industry Visit 1 (subject to availability)
  • Overnight stay at the hotel
Day 3 – Industry Exposure & Cultural Experience
  • Breakfast at the hotel
  • Industry Visit 2 (subject to availability)
  • Learn about innovation, sustainability, and global business practices
  • Visit Bugis Street and experience Singapore's local culture
  • Overnight stay at the hotel
Day 4 – Sentosa Island Experience
  • Breakfast at the hotel
  • Explore Sentosa Island
  • Free time to enjoy attractions and experiences
  • Optional visit to Universal Studios Singapore
  • Wings of Time Show
  • Overnight stay at the hotel
Day 5 – Singapore → Mumbai | Departure
  • Breakfast at the hotel
  • Check-out from the hotel
  • Free time for shopping and exploration
  • Transfer to Singapore Changi Airport
  • Arrive in Mumbai with valuable global exposure and unforgettable memories
