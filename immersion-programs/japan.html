Program Overview

Discover the Future of
Innovation & Precision

A country where precision, innovation, and tradition coexist, Japan offers a powerful learning environment for future global leaders.

Japan is one of the world’s most advanced economies, known for its excellence in manufacturing, technology, and management philosophies like Kaizen and Lean.

This program gives students a front-row seat to how global organizations operate with discipline, efficiency, and innovation — while understanding the cultural values that drive them.

Schedule

Indicative Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Tokyo & Cultural Heritage
  • Arrive in Tokyo and check into your hotel
  • Explore the Asakusa district, including Senso-ji Temple and Nakamise Shopping Street
  • Visit the Imperial Palace, the main residence of the Emperor of Japan
  • Explore the trendy Harajuku district and see the unique fashion styles
  • Visit the Nezu Museum, designed by Kengo Kuma
Day 2 – Tokyo | Academic Session & Industry Immersion
  • After breakfast, head for a visit to a university
  • Later, head for industry visit 1
  • In the evening, visit the beautiful Hamarikyu Gardens or the Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building for panoramic views of the city
Day 3 – Tokyo → Mt. Fuji → Shizuoka
  • After breakfast, check out and head to visit Mt. Fuji
  • Post lunch, transfer to Shizuoka
  • Overnight stay in Shizuoka
Day 4 – Shizuoka → Kyoto | Shinkansen Bullet Train
  • After breakfast, check-out and proceed for industry visit 2
  • Later transfer to Kyoto by bullet train (Shinkansen)
  • On reaching Kyoto, visit the Ryoan-ji Temple, famous for its Zen garden. Take a stroll through the beautiful Philosopher's Path
  • Later check-in at the hotel
Day 5 – Kyoto → Osaka | Industry Visit & Dotonbori
  • After breakfast, check out and proceed for industry visit 3
  • Later transfer to Osaka
  • On reaching Osaka, visit the Osaka Castle, a majestic castle that played a crucial role in the unification of Japan
  • Explore the Dotonbori district, known for its vibrant nightlife, street food, and unique entertainment
  • In the evening, post-dinner check-in at the hotel
Day 6 – Osaka | Universal Studios Japan
  • After breakfast, the day is free to visit Universal Studios Japan in Osaka
Day 7 – Osaka → Departure
  • After breakfast, check-out and enjoy free time
  • Later head to the airport for the scheduled departure flight
