Special Learning Tours - International Conference

VEPL facilitates international conferences and global immersion programs that provide participants with valuable exposure to emerging technologies, innovation ecosystems, industry practices, research trends, and cross-cultural learning experiences. These programs are designed for students, professionals, academicians, entrepreneurs, and industry leaders who aspire to expand their global perspective and professional network.

Our international conference programs combine academic learning, industry interaction, networking opportunities, and experiential travel into one enriching experience. Participants get the opportunity to attend keynote sessions, expert talks, panel discussions, workshops, and collaborative forums led by renowned global experts and industry professionals.

Program Highlights

  • Participation in prestigious international conferences and summits
  • Exposure to global trends in AI, technology, business, education, and innovation
  • Interactive keynote sessions, workshops, and panel discussions
  • Networking opportunities with international delegates and industry experts
  • Industry visits and experiential learning opportunities
  • Cultural exposure and global collaboration experiences
  • Professional development and knowledge enhancement
  • End-to-end travel and event coordination by VEPL

Inclusions

Depending on the program and destination, inclusions may cover:

  • Conference registration and participation
  • Hotel accommodation
  • Airport transfers and local transportation
  • Conference venue transfers
  • Visa guidance and assistance
  • Selected meals during the program
  • Travel kits and event materials
  • On-ground support by experienced coordinators
  • Guided immersion and networking activities
